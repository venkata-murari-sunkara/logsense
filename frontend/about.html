About

LogSense

HDFS writes millions of log lines; real failures are rare and buried in routine activity. LogSense flags the blocks that look wrong, then explains why by pointing to similar past incidents — so engineers can act on the result instead of guessing.

Try it: Analyze a block

How it works

1

Detect

A classifier flags the block as Normal or Anomaly.

2

Retrieve

Similar past incidents are pulled from the historical dataset.

3

Explain

The AI explains the verdict using those incidents as evidence.

4

Draft

For a likely anomaly, a ticket can be drafted on demand.

Does evidence help?
